A zero-barrier digital health assistant connecting rural mothers to evidence-based AI care.

⚙️ Engineering & AI Architecture

Robust Tech Stack & RAG Pipeline

Modern stack combining vector retrieval with dual failover safety engines.

Frontend & App
Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Material UI symbols.
Database & RAG
Supabase (PostgreSQL), `pgvector` extension for 384d PDF document embeddings.
AI & Embeddings
OpenAI `gpt-4o-mini`, `text-embedding-3-small`, and local MiniLM embeddings.
Dual RAG Strategy: Vector similarity RPC (`match_documents`) ➔ Keyword fallback query ➔ Offline `medical-database.ts` fallback engine.

Patient Workflow Walkthrough

From symptom entry in a rural Chak to clinic token reservation.

Voice / Text Input

Step 2
RAG PDF Match

Step 3
Direct Guidance
Dr. Ayesha gives immediate kick-count advice and emergency warning signs.
Step 4
Nearest BHU Route
Shows distance (e.g. 2.4 km away), Google Maps route, and reserves token.

🛡️ Clinical Governance

Medical Safety & Ethics Guardrails

Building AI healthcare with strict clinical boundary rules.

🔒 Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer
Sehat AI clearly states it is an educational tool, preserving the doctor-patient relationship while triaging urgent risks.
📄 RAG Vector Source Attributions
Every AI response links directly to its clinical PDF source chunk to prevent hallucination.
🔐 Row Level Security (RLS)
Strict Supabase Postgres policies protecting patient medical history and personal data.
🚨 Red-Flag Safety Overrides
Critical symptoms automatically trigger 1122 ambulance shortcuts and red warning banners.
